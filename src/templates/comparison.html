🛡️ Rigorous Machine Learning Methodology & Data Integrity

🔒

Strict Data Leakage Prevention

Images were partitioned into stratified training (70%), validation (15%), and test (15%) splits prior to fitting any preprocessing or normalization components.

🔄

Data Augmentation Isolation

Random rotations, horizontal/vertical flips, and color adjustments were strictly isolated to the training split. Validation and test sets were evaluated on clean, unaugmented images.

⚖️

Balanced Class Weighting

Weighted cross-entropy loss functions were applied to penalize misclassifications proportionally, maintaining calibrated decision boundaries.

🎯

Sensitivity-First Model Selection

In accordance with oncological dermatology standards, final model selection prioritized Sensitivity to minimize false negatives (missed melanomas).
